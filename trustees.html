How governance works

From oversight to member outcomes.

The governance model brings Board oversight, professional advice and day-to-day administration together around members' long-term interests.

01 · Direction

Board oversight

Sets strategic priorities, approves key policies and monitors Fund performance.

02 · Assurance

Committees & advisers

Provides specialist input on investment, audit, risk, governance and compliance.

03 · Service

Secretariat support

Coordinates Fund administration, communication and practical member support.
