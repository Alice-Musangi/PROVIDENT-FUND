Critical illness benefit

Support at a serious diagnosis.

A dedicated lump-sum benefit to help members focus on recovery and the practical costs that can follow illness.

+

What is the Critical Illness Benefit?

The Critical Illness Benefit provides a lump-sum payment upon the first diagnosis of a covered critical illness. The payment helps members manage medical costs, recovery expenses and loss of income during serious illness.

Benefit amount

Main Member
35% of the Sum Assured
Spouse
KES 300,000

Illnesses covered

Conditions included in the benefit.

The benefit is payable on the first confirmed diagnosis of a covered condition, in line with Fund policy terms.

  • Cancer
  • Heart Attack
  • Stroke
  • Kidney (Renal) Failure
  • Major Organ Transplant
  • Coronary Artery Surgery
  • Heart Valve Replacement
  • Paralysis
  • Blindness
  • Multiple Sclerosis
  • Parkinson’s Disease
  • Alzheimer’s Disease
  • Motor Neurone Disease
  • Coma
  • Muscular Dystrophy
  • Accidental Brain Damage
  • Rheumatoid Disease

New Benefits — Effective 2026

Expanded support for families.

Two additional benefits designed to recognise important needs within our member community.

New∞

Child Living with Disability

Children living with disability remain covered with no maximum age limit upon submission of a valid disability certificate from the National Council of Persons Living with Disability (NCPWD).

New+

Stillbirth Benefit

A lump-sum payment of KES 25,000 is available for losses occurring from 28 weeks of conception, subject to policy terms and required medical documentation.

Important reminders

Keep your cover current.

These practical steps help the Fund assess and support a claim without avoidable delays.

iDeclaration and nomination
  • Declare all spouses, children and parents.
  • Benefits are not payable for undeclared dependants.
  • Keep nomination forms updated.
!Claims and critical illness
  • Critical illness benefits are payable only upon the first confirmed diagnosis.
  • Notify the Fund promptly and submit the required medical documentation when making a claim.
