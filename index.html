Provident fund essentials

Understand today. Plan tomorrow with confidence.

SETSPF facts, supported by general member guidance from the Retirement Benefits Authority.

01

How your Fund works

Contributions made towards your retirement are credited to the Fund and invested under the oversight of the Trustees, with support from appointed administrators, investment managers, custodians, insurers and auditors.

02

How your savings grow

You contribute 10% of basic salary and your employer contributes 10% of basic salary, both inclusive of NSSF Tier II. These contributions, any Additional Voluntary Contributions and investment returns build your accumulated benefit, after permitted costs.

AVCs are encouraged and may qualify for tax relief within the applicable KES 30,000 monthly limit.

03

What happens when you leave employment

SETSPF provides for benefits on resignation before age 50, retirement, ill-health retirement, death and qualifying emigration. The amount accessible, preserved or transferable depends on applicable law and the Fund’s Trust Deed and Rules.

04

Your rights and responsibilities

You have a right to Fund information, annual benefit statements and fair treatment. Review your statements, provide accurate information, declare eligible dependants and keep your beneficiary nomination current.

Fund-specific facts reflect the SETSPF Members Presentation dated 18 June 2026. Tax treatment, exit options and individual benefits remain subject to current law and the SETSPF Trust Deed and Rules.

Critical illness benefit

Support at a serious diagnosis.

A dedicated lump-sum benefit to help members focus on recovery and the practical costs that can follow illness.

+

What is the Critical Illness Benefit?

The Critical Illness Benefit provides a lump-sum payment upon the first diagnosis of a covered critical illness. The payment helps members manage medical costs, recovery expenses and loss of income during serious illness.

New Benefits — Effective 2026

Expanded support for families.

Two additional benefits designed to recognise important needs within our member community.

New∞

Child Living with Disability

Children living with disability remain covered with no maximum age limit upon submission of a valid disability certificate from the National Council of Persons Living with Disability (NCPWD).

New+

Stillbirth Benefit

A lump-sum payment of KES 25,000 is available for losses occurring from 28 weeks of conception, subject to policy terms and required medical documentation.
