Who We Are

Rooted in stewardship. Driven by your future.

For 36 years, SETSPF has walked alongside members of the Strathmore family—preserving retirement savings, strengthening financial confidence and supporting members at every stage.

36years of safeguarding your retirement

Our guiding purpose

A Fund built around members.

Every contribution, every return and every informed decision should move our members closer to a peaceful, dignified retirement.

SETSPF is a staff provident fund established by the Strathmore Educational Trust Registered Trustees. We combine prudent investment, accountable governance, clear communication and responsive service to protect and grow members’ retirement savings.

Our Story

Walking alongside members since 1990.

Established in January 1990 by the Strathmore Educational Trust Registered Trustees, SETSPF has grown into a strong and resilient retirement partner.

Our story is one of steady stewardship: members saving consistently, Trustees serving responsibly, and professional partners helping the Fund create long-term value.

1990

The Fund is established

SETSPF begins serving eligible employees within the Strathmore family.

2025

Strong investment performance

The Fund records a 17.24% annual return through disciplined investment and diversification.

2026

A new chapter begins

The 2025–2030 Strategic Plan launches as the Fund marks 36 years of stewardship.

Today

Growing together

KES 3B+ in fund value and 1,132 active members as at 30 July 2026.
