What Is the Critical Illness Benefit?

The Critical Illness Benefit provides a lump-sum payment to a member upon first diagnosis of a covered critical illness.

The benefit is designed to provide you with financial support when you need it most, so you can focus on your health and recovery .

The money is paid directly to you to help manage medical costs, recovery expenses and loss of income during serious illness.

New Benefits (Effective 2026)

New Benefits (Effective 2026)

New∞

Child Living with Disability

Children living with disability can now remain covered without a maximum age limit, subject to provision of a valid disability certificate from the National Council of Persons Living with Disability (NCPWD). This ensures continued protection for children who depend on long-term care.

New+

Loss After 28 Weeks (Stillbirth Benefit)

The Stillbirth Benefit provides a lump-sum payment of KES 25,000 to help cover last expenses in the event of loss occurring from 28 weeks of conception. This benefit is intended to offer financial support to the family during a difficult time, subject to policy terms and required medical documentation.

Important reminders

Important reminders

To ensure that you and your loved ones fully benefit, please take note of the following critical requirements:

1Declare Your Dependents

All spouses, children, and parents must be formally declared .

Important: No benefits will be paid to undeclared dependents.

2Update Your Nomination Forms

Keep your nomination forms up to date to reflect your current wishes.

This ensures that benefits are paid to the correct beneficiaries in a timely manner.

3Critical Illness Declarations

Critical illness benefits are payable only upon first confirmed diagnosis of a covered illness.

Members must notify us promptly upon diagnosis to initiate the claims process and provide all required medical documentation
