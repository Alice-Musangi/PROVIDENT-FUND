Communication & Engagement · 6–8 April 2026

Clear conversations that foster financial independence.

One statement. One conversation. One step closer to understanding and owning your retirement journey.

Member educationPublished April 2026← Back to latest news

There is something different about a conversation that is just between two people. No raised hands. No waiting your turn. No holding back a question because it feels too personal for a room full of colleagues. Just you, an expert, your retirement statement laid out on the table, and the freedom to ask exactly what you have been wondering about for years.

That is precisely what over 100 members of staff experienced at Strathmore University from 6 to 8 April 2026, as the Strathmore Educational Trust Staff Provident Fund ran its #TukoFonnaIRR Clinic: three days of dedicated, one-to-one sessions where every member could sit down, open their statement, and finally understand what it means.

No two conversations were the same. That was the whole point.

Your statement, line by line

Most of us have received a retirement fund statement at some point, skimmed the total, filed it away, and quietly hoped the numbers were heading in the right direction. The IRR Clinic was built to end that habit.

Each member sat down privately with a facilitator and worked through their own retirement statement in detail: their contributions, fund balance, Internal Rate of Return, and what all of it means for the retirement they are building toward.

For many, it was a revelation. Numbers that had always felt abstract suddenly had names and timelines attached to them. The IRR, often one of the least understood figures on a fund statement, was explained not just in technical terms, but in real, personal ones.

  • What rate of return has your money been earning?
  • Is it keeping pace with inflation?
  • What does that mean for you, given your age, contribution level and retirement goals?

These are the questions that a statement alone cannot answer. But a conversation can.

The questions nobody usually gets to ask

When a conversation is private, people get honest. Members asked why a figure looked different from the previous year, whether they were contributing enough, what an earlier retirement could mean, and what happens to savings when an employment situation changes.

These are the questions that matter most when you are trying to plan a life. They deserve real, personalised answers rather than a footnote in a brochure.

Personalised attention

We listened, guided, and where necessary, gently challenged assumptions. The result was not just information. It was clarity.

Retirement is not a collective experience; it is one of the most personal financial journeys a person will ever take. Your statement tells your story. Your contributions, growth rate and projected balance at retirement will not look exactly like your colleague's, and should not be treated as though they do.

By sitting with each member individually and walking through their statement page by page, the Fund reinforced that retirement matters personally, not just as a line in a report.

A commitment that goes beyond three days

This clinic is not a once-off gesture. It sits within the Fund's 2025-2030 Strategic Plan, which places member education, personalised engagement and financial transparency at its core. The vision is a membership that does not just contribute to the Fund, but truly understands and owns its retirement journey.

Over 100 staff left the sessions better informed, more confident and, perhaps most importantly, more in control of their own financial futures. Look out for upcoming one-to-one advisory sessions and member engagement forums near you.
