Website guidance

Terms & Conditions

Use of this website

This website provides general information and access to SETSPF resources. Information published here does not replace the Fund’s governing documents, applicable legislation or professional advice.

Accuracy and availability

SETSPF aims to keep information accurate and current. Content, services and downloadable resources may be updated when required.

Responsible use

Users must not interfere with the website, attempt unauthorized access or misuse any information or resource made available through it.

Questions

For clarification, email trustees@strathmore.edu.

Personal information

Privacy Policy

Information we receive

Information may be provided when you contact the Fund, submit a form or request assistance. This can include contact details and information needed to respond to your request.

How information is used

Information is used to administer enquiries, provide member services, maintain appropriate records and meet lawful obligations.

Protection and sharing

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect personal information. Information is only shared where necessary for Fund administration, where authorized, or where required by law.

Your questions

Privacy enquiries and correction requests may be sent to trustees@strathmore.edu.
