Strategy 2025–2030 · 13 March 2026

A new chapter: safeguarding members’ retirement by promoting financial freedom.

Our mission, five strategic pillars and core values now provide one clear direction for the Fund.

Fund updatePublished 13 March 2026← Back to latest news

On the morning of 13 March 2026, the Strathmore Educational Trust Staff Provident Fund officially launched its 2025-2030 Strategic Plan. Current and former trustees gathered for a milestone that felt less like a boardroom meeting and more like a homecoming and a celebration.

The presence of former trustees was a quiet but powerful endorsement: the Fund is in good hands, and it is heading somewhere worth going.

Built around you

At the centre of the new strategy is a commitment that every member deserves to hold the Fund to:

Safeguarding members' retirement by promoting financial freedom.

This is not a slogan. It is the standard against which every decision over the next five years will be measured: from how savings are invested, to how quickly benefits are received, to how often members hear from the Fund.

The plan is structured around five pillars: Investments; Audit, Risk and Compliance; Communication and Member Engagement; Operations; and Governance. Together, they represent a Fund that is not standing still.

What's changing

On the investment side, the Fund is moving beyond its traditional reliance on government securities. Real estate, private equity and offshore equities are now part of the picture, with a target return of inflation plus 3.5% annually.

For members who have sometimes felt kept at arm's length, the Communication pillar brings real-time account access, WhatsApp and SMS updates, open engagement forums and annual satisfaction surveys. Members will be able to understand how their Fund is performing, clearly and regularly.

Operationally, the Fund has set a target to process benefit payments within 15 days, supported by a focus on clearer processes and timely service.

On governance, trustee term limits, board evaluations and succession planning are embedded in how the Fund is led, supporting strong and accountable leadership into the future.

Looking after the whole member

The launch introduced a Post-Retirement Medical Fund (PRMF), recognising that a dignified retirement is not only about money in the bank: healthcare security after retirement matters too.

The road ahead

Progress against the strategy will be tracked, measured and reported back to members at annual meetings. The Abridged Strategic Plan is available for review, and member feedback will continue to shape how the Fund grows.

Thirty-five years of stewardship have brought the Fund to this point. The 2025-2030 Strategic Plan is the promise of what comes next: a retirement future built on transparency, ambition and an unwavering commitment to every single member.

The next chapter has begun. And it was built with you in mind.
